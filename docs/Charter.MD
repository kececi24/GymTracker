## 3. Project Scope
### Project domain
Fitness Tech & Digital Health application targeted to commercial gym members, powerlifters, bodybuilders and general sports enthusiasts.

Key users are trainees and athletes who are individuals executing workout programs, logging sets, reps, weights, monitoring strength, and body composition progress over time.


### In-Scope 
* Workout Routine Builder & Program Scheduling 
* Live Workout Weight Tracking
* Exercise Library & Movement Database
* Performance Analytics and Workout Feedback

### Out-of-Scope 
* Calorie & Dietary Tracking
* Live User Health Tracking and Related Hardware 
* Gym Membership Management or Billing
